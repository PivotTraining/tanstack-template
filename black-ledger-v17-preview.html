The Black Ledger Method

A path, not a pile of content.

The product is built around progression. Learners are assessed, placed, taught, practiced, checked, and advanced through a deliberate sequence instead of being dropped into a content library.

01

Assess what the learner already knows.

Experience, chart literacy, order types, risk habits, strategy maturity, and goals establish the starting point.

Placement
02

Teach one competency at a time.

Lessons are large, readable, focused, and paired with examples instead of being compressed into dashboard cards.

Instruction
03

Require application, not passive watching.

The Desk turns concepts into simulated trading decisions, calculations, scenario work, and process review.

Practice
04

Measure understanding before advancement.

Knowledge checks and module exams create evidence of competency before the next stage unlocks.

The Practice Desk

Grade the decision, not the luck.

A profitable simulated trade can still be a poor decision. Black Ledger separates process quality from outcome so risk discipline is rewarded even when the market does not cooperate.

A

Defined risk before entry

Position size, invalidation, and maximum acceptable loss are established before the simulated trade.

Process
B

Executed according to plan

The system tracks whether the learner honored the planned entry, stop, and exit logic.

Discipline
C

Reviewed the decision afterward

The journal captures reasoning, mistakes, emotional interference, and what should change next time.

Reflection
