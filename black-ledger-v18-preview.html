The Practice Desk

Grade the decision, not the luck.

A winning simulated trade can still be a poor decision. Black Ledger separates process quality from outcome so risk discipline is rewarded.

A

Define risk before entry.

Position size, invalidation, and maximum acceptable loss come first.

Process
B

Execute according to plan.

The system tracks whether the learner honored the planned entry, stop, and exit logic.

Discipline
C

Review the decision afterward.

The journal captures reasoning, mistakes, and what should change next time.
